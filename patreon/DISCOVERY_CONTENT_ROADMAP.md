## Internal-link pattern

At the end of a free post:

- link to one related free post
- name the next guided member activity clearly
- mention eligible tiers accurately
- avoid pressure language

At the beginning of a paid continuation:

- link back to the free foundation
- state what new practice begins here
- preserve access for Educator Studio when the content is also for AP Japanese Achiever

## Originality rule

Every practice text, prompt, example response, worksheet, thumbnail, and explanation must be original. External sources may inform facts and teaching context but must not be copied, repackaged, or redistributed.

## Review order

For each planned article:

1. Confirm the primary audience and search question.
2. Complete SEARCH_METADATA_TEMPLATE.md.
3. Verify changing facts against current primary sources.
4. Draft with ARTICLE_TEMPLATE.md and PATREON_RULES.md.
5. Review tags with TAG_TAXONOMY.md.
6. Run PATREON_QA_CHECKLIST.md.
7. Ask the founder immediately before Save, Update, or Publish.
