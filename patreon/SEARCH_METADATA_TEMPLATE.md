## Title check

- [ ] Names the real topic
- [ ] Shows a concrete benefit or task
- [ ] Includes AP Japanese when relevant
- [ ] Avoids exaggerated promises
- [ ] Remains readable without the series label
- [ ] Does not imply College Board affiliation or endorsement

## Opening check

Within roughly the first 80 words:

- [ ] Names the reader’s situation
- [ ] Uses the primary phrase once, naturally
- [ ] Explains what the reader will learn or do
- [ ] Acknowledges a realistic worry
- [ ] Sounds warm and human

## Structure check

- [ ] Short Heading 3 subheads
- [ ] Blank line before each subhead
- [ ] One main idea per section
- [ ] Bullets for steps, choices, or checks
- [ ] English and やさしい日本語 are clearly separated
- [ ] A concrete student or classroom example is included
- [ ] The article is useful before any paywall
